Data handling

The gateway does not store prompts, answers or content hashes. Request content is sent to the selected supplier to generate a response. Supplier data policies apply separately; this is not a supplier zero-retention promise.

Account email, verification state, orders and payment references, usage and operator audits support service operation and reconciliation. API keys are shown once and stored as digests. The published privacy policy must specify retention periods and data-request procedures.

Credit & refunds

Credit is for using this service, not withdrawal, transfer or unlimited usage. Credit is recorded in integer microUSD, with 1 USD = 1,000,000 microUSD. Cash payments and service credit are separate accounting units.

Requests reserve credit before settling at the frozen price. Uncertain costs retain their hold for review; a timeout is not automatically treated as free.

The initial technical workflow supports one full refund per purchase order. This is not an unconditional refund promise. Eligibility, tax, fees, time limits and disputes must be defined in final refund terms and reconciled against the payment provider's confirmed outcome.
